One interval width per look, multiplied together — a cell survives only if every look saw structure there. What counts as a look is the choice below. The rail is the method in order: each look on its own, then × all of them. The multiply is where buildings turn into outlines and tree crowns into filled blobs, and that difference is what separates them with no classification field.
